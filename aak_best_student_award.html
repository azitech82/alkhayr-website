About the Award

The AAK Best Student Award is a yearly recognition for students who demonstrate excellent character (akhlaq), strong academic effort, and meaningful contribution to the AAK community.

Akhlaq

Adab, discipline, and integrity.

Academics

Consistency, progress, and effort.

Service

Helping others and leading by example.

How It Works

Parents and teachers can recommend a student. The final decision considers character, effort, and contribution across the year.
